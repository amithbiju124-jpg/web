About the Department

Established in 2004, the Department of Computer Science and Engineering offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes built around practical, project-based learning. Our students work in industry-standard labs from their first semester.

Address queries to the department office.
Office hours: 9:00 am to 4:30 pm, Monday to Friday.

Intake figures are approximate and may change each academic year.

Vision and Mission

Vision

To be a centre of excellence in computing education and research.

Mission

  • Deliver rigorous, industry-relevant teaching.
  • Encourage research, innovation and open-source contribution.
  • Build ethical, socially responsible engineers.
